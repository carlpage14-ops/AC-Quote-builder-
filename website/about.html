About Engineered Air

A North East AC company built by engineers — not salesmen.

We started Engineered Air because we kept seeing the same thing on site: oversized systems, lazy installs, missing paperwork. We wanted to do it properly.

Our story

From a single van in Gateshead to a regional specialist.

Engineered Air was founded in 2011 by two refrigeration engineers who'd spent a decade between them working on big contracts for the national chains. We noticed something simple — the work was rarely "engineered." Systems were sized off rules of thumb. Pipework was untidy. Commissioning was rushed.

We started small: one van, residential splits and small office jobs in Gateshead and Newcastle. We obsessed about the details — clean pipework, considerate working, paperwork that didn't need chasing. Word got around.

Today we're a team of directly-employed engineers covering everything from a £900 home install to multi-floor VRF projects across the North East. What hasn't changed: we're still engineers first, and every quote you get comes from the person who'd actually fit it.

How we work

Three principles. Every job. No exceptions.

01

Engineered, not estimated

Every system is sized with a proper heat-load calculation. No oversizing to be safe, no undersizing to win the job.

02

Tidy on, tidy off

Dust sheets out, debris bagged, surfaces wiped. You shouldn't be able to tell we were there — except the room's now cooler.

03

Direct, no fluff

We tell you what you need, not what we can sell you. If you don't need air con — we'll say so.
