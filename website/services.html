01 · Install

Installation & system design

From a single split unit in a home office to a 30-unit VRF system across a Cat B office fit-out, every Engineered Air install starts with a proper survey and a CIBSE-compliant heat load calculation.

Wall-mounted splits

Quiet, efficient single-room cooling. Ideal for home offices, smaller meeting rooms and bedrooms.

Cassette systems

Recessed into ceilings for a clean, four-way airflow. Perfect for offices, retail and hospitality.

Ducted & concealed

For premium spaces where the kit must vanish — ducted via void with discreet grilles.

Floor-standing

When wall and ceiling mounting isn't possible. High-output units for large open spaces.
